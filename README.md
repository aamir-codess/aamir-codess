## 🚀 Featured Project: Qists Manager

An installment tracking app for small shops that sell products on monthly payments — customer management, automatic payment schedules, WhatsApp reminders, and full account-level security.

1.**🔗 Live app:** https://qists-manager.lovable.app
**📄 Repo:** [qists-manager](https://github.com/aamir-codess/qists-manager)

2.**🔗 Live app:**https://salesights.lovable.app
**📄 Repo:**[sale-sights](https://github.com/aamir-codess/salesights)

**What makes this more than a demo:** I manually security-tested it after building it, and found a real **IDOR (Insecure Direct Object Reference) vulnerability** — one shop owner could view another's customer data by editing a URL. I found it, diagnosed it, fixed it (took two attempts to get the fix right), and verified the fix on the live published app. Full write-up is in the repo's `TESTING.md`.

Other issues found and fixed during testing: payment overpayment handling, schedule display accuracy, and login brute-force protection.
